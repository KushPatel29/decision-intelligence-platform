# Segmentation

_Generated by `scripts/build_model_cards.py` from the latest pipeline run._

## Purpose

Describe behavioural groups for reporting and analyst work.

## Method

Rule-based RFM segments; K-Means (5 clusters) and a Gaussian mixture whose size is chosen by BIC on log-scaled frequency, spend, distance, period mix, digital activity and recency.

## Evidence

K-Means silhouette 0.214, Davies-Bouldin 1.32, bootstrap ARI 0.99-0.99. Gaussian mixture: 8 components, silhouette 0.109. K-Means vs mixture ARI 0.44; K-Means vs RFM 0.37.

## Limits

Segments are descriptive; the plan targets by estimated incremental value, not segment.
