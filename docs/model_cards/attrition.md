# Declining use (attrition)

_Generated by `scripts/build_model_cards.py` from the latest pipeline run._

## Purpose

Probability that trips in the next 90 days fall below half of the prior 90 days - an early warning before full inactivity.

## Target and population

Historically active customers.

## Selection

Logistic regression, histogram gradient boosting and XGBoost are fitted on monthly snapshots from July 2024 to January 2025. The champion has the lowest validation Brier score (April 2025), then a Platt calibrator is fitted on that fold. Validation leaderboard (uncalibrated):

| Algorithm | AUC | Brier | ECE |
|---|---|---|---|
| logistic | 0.592 | 0.1287 | 0.044 |
| hist_gradient_boosting | 0.720 | 0.1127 | 0.043 |
| xgboost | 0.718 | 0.1127 | 0.041 |

Champion: **hist_gradient_boosting**.

## Use and limits

A descriptive score for segmentation and reporting. It is not used to target offers: the policy evaluation shows that travel propensity correlates negatively with the value an offer creates. Synthetic population; no protected characteristics are used or available.

## Monitoring and retraining

Prediction PSI against the July test scores and feature PSI each batch; review above 0.2. Retrain only after matured labels confirm a drop in AUC or calibration, and only through the same validation-then-test promotion.
