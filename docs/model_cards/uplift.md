## Purpose

Estimate, for every customer and offer, the incremental trips by travel period an offer causes; the offer's terms turn them into expected value and cost for the optimizer.

## Data

The July 2025 10-arm customer-randomised trial (blocked on 90-day trips and trend). Learners fit on the training fold, are chosen on the validation fold and are scored on untouched test customers.

## Carry-over

Days 31-90 margin per dollar of in-window gross contribution, regression-adjusted: discounts 0.38 (90% CI 0.07-0.67), loyalty 0.49 (90% CI -0.14-0.94). Planning uses the 20th percentile.

## Calibration (diagnostic, not applied)

A BLP recalibration would move the mean value bias from $-4.49 to $-3.86 per customer-offer and increase bias for 0 of 9 offers in this run. It is not applied: when it was first tested it increased bias for 7 of 8 offers, and a 15% validation fold is too small to estimate the level reliably.

## Prediction against truth

The optimized plan was predicted to create $42,649 and in simulation truly creates $64,774: here the estimates are conservative in level (the learners under-state value by $4.49 per customer-offer on average), which outweighs the winner's curse that selecting on noisy estimates always adds. The plan depends on the ranking, not the level; the level is validated by the randomised policy trial, not assumed.
