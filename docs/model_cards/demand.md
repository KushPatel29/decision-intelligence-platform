# 30-day demand forecast

_Generated by `scripts/build_model_cards.py` from the latest pipeline run._

## Purpose

Baseline trips by zone, travel period and day for the October window; feeds capacity.

## Method

Weekly fixed origins, each forecasting the next 30 days from origin-time information only. The learned model predicts a multiplicative correction to the same-weekday mean; it competes with the same-weekday mean and a seasonal naive on validation, must beat the same-weekday baseline on test, and is refit on all history before forecasting October.

## Held-out result

Serving: **ratio_boosting**. MAE 43.8 trips per cell-day against 50.2 (same-weekday mean) and 54.8 (seasonal naive), over 9 held-out origins. Split-conformal 90% intervals covered 92%.

## Limits

Weather enters only through history, not a weather forecast. Hour and direction are disaggregated with historical shares, not modelled.
