# Account anomaly review

_Generated by `scripts/build_model_cards.py` from the latest pipeline run._

## Purpose

Queue unusual accounts (cloned transponders, night travel far from home) for review.

## Method

Isolation Forest on log-scaled velocity, night-travel and zone-spread features with a fixed review capacity; a robust z-score detector is the baseline. Review signal, not fraud adjudication.

## Evidence against planted anomalies

| Detector | Caught | Planted | Precision | Recall | PR-AUC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Isolation Forest | 30 | 30 | 24% | 100% | 0.403 |
| Robust z-score | 11 | 30 | 7% | 37% | 0.123 |

## Limits

A review signal with a fixed capacity, not fraud adjudication.
