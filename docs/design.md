# Decision product design

Subject: a synthetic electronic toll-road campaign planning desk. Audience: marketing, revenue and transportation analysts. Main task: inspect incremental economics and capacity, then review a proposed campaign.

Palette: corridor ink #16374a, roadway teal #006d77, planning blue #245d92, clear sky #edf4f7, paper white #ffffff, review amber #a95600. Teal means campaign value; blue means baseline demand; amber signals assumptions or review. Colour never supplies the only label.

Typography: Segoe UI with Calibri fallback for familiar Windows analytical work. Large headings establish the decision area; tabular numbers align financial comparisons. No external font or chart downloads.

Layout: left-hand decision navigation, a wide planning workspace, then evidence tables. The standalone overview uses one six-stop corridor diagram, with the rest kept quiet. Streamlit adapts the same identity to its native accessible widgets.

```
Corridor                                    Synthetic simulation
Decision areas | Decision + four outcome measures
               | Road zones / trend + constraint evidence
               | Scenario controls / recommendation table
```

Review against the brief: identical generic cards were replaced with a corridor capacity diagram and financial constraint evidence. Charts stop at the decision cutoff; generated future data appears only in the documented data volume and labels. Responsive tables scroll instead of hiding columns. Focus is visible and motion is unnecessary.
